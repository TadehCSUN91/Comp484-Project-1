Workout Routines

A simple 3-day routine to get started, or use it as a template for your own split.

Day 1

  • Squat (Barbell) — 4 sets · 5 reps
  • Triceps Pushdown — 3 sets · 12-15 reps
  • Pull Up — 3 sets · 8-10 reps
  • Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell) — 3 sets · 8-10 reps
  • Dumbbell Step Up — 3 sets · 10-12 reps
  • Lateral Raise (Dumbbell) — 3 sets · 10-15 reps

Day 2

  • Deadlift (Barbell) — 3 sets · 5 reps
  • Overhead Press (Barbell) — 4 sets · 5 reps
  • Chin Up — 3 sets · 8-10 reps
  • Farmers Walk — 3 sets
  • Seated Leg Curl (Machine) — 3 sets · 10-12 reps
  • Bicep Curl (Dumbbell) — 3 sets · 10-12 reps

Day 3

  • Bench Press (Barbell) — 4 sets · 5 reps
  • Bent Over Row (Barbell) — 4 sets · 5 reps
  • Split Squat (Dumbbell) — 3 sets · 8-10 reps
  • Neutral Grip Pullup — 3 sets · 8-10 reps
  • Hammer Curl (Dumbbell) — 3 sets · 10-12 reps
