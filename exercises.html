Exercise Library

Search or browse common exercises used in RackIt routines.

Squat (Barbell)

A compound lower-body exercise targeting the quads, glutes, and hamstrings.

Keep your chest up, knees tracking over your toes, and drive through your heels.

Bulgarian Split Squat (Dumbbell)

A single-leg exercise that builds strength and balance in the quads and glutes.

Also known as: BulgarianSplitSquat in some workout apps.

Keep most of your weight on your front leg and your torso upright throughout the movement.

Deadlift (Barbell)

A full-body pulling movement that builds strength in the back, glutes, and hamstrings.

Keep the bar close to your shins, brace your core, and drive through your heels as you stand up.

Overhead Press (Barbell)

A shoulder-focused pressing movement that also builds core and upper-back stability.

Brace your core, keep your ribs down, and press straight overhead without arching your lower back.

Bent Over Row (Barbell)

A back exercise that targets the lats, rhomboids, and rear delts.

Hinge at the hips, keep your back flat, and pull the bar toward your lower ribs.

Pull Up

A bodyweight pulling exercise that builds back and bicep strength.

Start from a full hang and pull until your chin clears the bar, avoiding excessive swinging.

Bicep Curl (Dumbbell)

An isolation exercise targeting the biceps.

Keep your elbows pinned to your sides and avoid swinging the weight up with momentum.

Lateral Raise (Dumbbell)

A shoulder isolation exercise that targets the medial deltoid.

Raise the dumbbells to shoulder height with a slight bend in the elbows, leading with your elbows.
